# Resolvable: short customer-support hiring assessment

Research checked 2 October 2026. All questions below are original, fictional work samples, not copied vendor test content. Vendor statements are product claims, not independent evidence of predictive validity. This is an implementation brief and pilot instrument, not a validated hiring test or legal advice.

## Recommendation

Build a configurable, ten-minute support work sample. Measure mechanics quickly, but give most weight to using a supplied policy correctly and composing an actual reply. Show question-level evidence, typing metrics, and a human-scored writing rubric. Never turn a prototype score into an automatic hiring or rejection decision.

## Default ten-minute blueprint

| Stage | Time | Tasks | Weight |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Instructions and keyboard check | 0:30 | Explain tools, timer, privacy and accommodation route; one unscored practice sentence | 0 |
| Spelling and grammar | 1:30 | Four concise selected-response items | 15 |
| Typing | 1:30 | Copy a realistic support passage | 15 |
| Practical problem solving | 2:00 | Three decisions using short supplied policy cards | 30 |
| Written reply | 4:00 | Compose one customer response using a supplied policy | 40 |
| Review and submit | 0:30 | Revisit selected responses/writing; typing is locked | 0 |
| **Total** | **10:00** | **Eight scored tasks** | **100** |

The default includes instructions and review. Clearly distinguish a standard ten-minute route from approved accommodated routes, which may take longer. Candidates can move on early. The timer must not advance while content loads. Save progress and offer a human-controlled technical-issue retake. Do not secretly penalize time spent reading.

Default item selection: L1–L4, T1, P1–P3, W1. This is a short screen, with coarse scores and limited sampling, not a comprehensive evaluation of English proficiency or customer-service potential.

### Customization controls

- Turn modules on/off; reorder; select difficulty; edit original questions, options, policy cards and answer explanations
- Edit spelling/grammar variant, typing passage, writing scenario and client-specific tone requirements
- Set module durations, with a visible sum and a standard-route warning/block above 600 seconds
- Set module weights totaling 100; show which results are unscored or await a reviewer
- Preview as candidate; create a new version on publication; freeze version, correct answers, rubric and timer settings for every submitted attempt
- Keep identical conditions for candidates in the same hiring round except documented accommodations; review equivalent-form difficulty before randomization
- Show tool policy explicitly: no external AI/assistance; supplied reference material allowed; spellcheck policy consistent across candidates. For raw mechanics turn browser spellcheck off, while recognizing that this cannot reliably disable all OS tools. For job-like writing, allow tools used on the job if the intended construct is tool-assisted writing
- Retain only necessary applicant data. Default demo data should be fictional; local demo persistence is not secure multi-user recruiting infrastructure

## Original item bank: spelling and grammar

Each item has one correct answer. Use A–D identifiers in storage; randomization must keep keys tied to identifiers, not screen position. Award one point per correct response and zero otherwise. Report the chosen option, correct option and concise explanation. Do not subtract points for guessing.

### L1: spelling / easy

Prompt: Choose the correctly spelled word to complete: “You will receive a ___ email after the return is approved.”

- A: confirmation
- B: comfirmation
- C: confimation
- D: confirmmation

Answer: A. Explanation: “Confirmation” is the correctly spelled noun.

### L2: agreement / easy

Prompt: Which sentence is grammatically correct?

- A: Your replacement items has been dispatched
- B: Your replacement items have been dispatched
- C: Your replacement items is been dispatched
- D: Your replacement items was dispatched today

Answer: B. Explanation: Plural “items” takes “have.”

### L3: apostrophes / medium

Prompt: Choose the sentence with correct apostrophe use.

- A: Its important to check the customers address
- B: It's important to check the customer's address
- C: Its' important to check the customer's address
- D: It's important to check the customers' address, when referring to one customer

Answer: B. Explanation: “It's” means “it is”; “customer's” indicates one customer's address. Option D explicitly refers to one customer and is therefore incorrect.

### L4: professional correction / easy

Prompt: Which version is both clear and grammatically correct?

- A: We have received your request and will reply within one business day
- B: We has received your request and will reply within one business day
- C: We have receive your request and will reply within one business day
- D: We received your request and will replies within one business day

Answer: A. Explanation: “Have received” and “will reply” use the correct verb forms.

### L5: spelling / medium

Prompt: Choose the correctly spelled word: “I ___ for the delay.”

- A: apoligize
- B: apologise
- C: appologise
- D: apolagize

Answer: B. Explanation: “Apologise” is correct British spelling. “Apologize” is also acceptable in free-text answers; do not score legitimate regional variation as an error.

### L6: tense / medium

Prompt: Choose the clearest correct sentence.

- A: Yesterday, I check your account and send a replacement
- B: Yesterday, I checked your account and sent a replacement
- C: Yesterday, I checked your account and sending a replacement
- D: Yesterday, I have check your account and sent a replacement

Answer: B. Explanation: Both actions occurred in the past: “checked” and “sent.”

### L7: their/there / easy

Prompt: Select the correct words: “The customers can update ___ delivery details from ___ account page.”

- A: there / their
- B: their / there
- C: their / their
- D: they're / their

Answer: C. Explanation: Both blanks show possession and require “their.”

### L8: punctuation / medium

Prompt: Choose the correctly punctuated version.

- A: Your refund is approved it may take five business days to appear
- B: Your refund is approved, it may take five business days to appear
- C: Your refund is approved. It may take five business days to appear
- D: Your refund is approved it, may take five business days to appear

Answer: C. Explanation: Two independent clauses can be separated into sentences. In this item, the other choices are run-ons, a comma splice, or a misplaced comma.

## Original item bank: practical problem solving

Use the policy card beside the question. Test applying information, not memorizing an unstated retailer's policy. One point per correct response. The P1–P3 module score is percent correct; its weighted contribution is 30 points maximum.

### P1: delayed order / easy

Policy card: “If tracking has not updated for three business days, open a carrier investigation. Investigations take up to two business days. Do not promise a replacement or a delivery date until the investigation is complete.”

Customer: “My order has not moved in tracking for four business days. Can you guarantee it will arrive tomorrow?”

What is the best next step?

- A: Guarantee tomorrow's delivery to reassure the customer
- B: Open a carrier investigation and explain its two-business-day timeframe
- C: Ask the customer to wait another week without taking action
- D: Mark the ticket resolved because the carrier has the parcel

Answer: B. Explanation: The threshold is met; investigate, set the stated timeframe, and avoid an unsupported guarantee.

### P2: duplicate charge / medium

Policy card: “A pending card authorization is not a completed charge. Check transaction status before requesting a refund. If two completed charges are confirmed for one order, escalate to billing with both transaction IDs. Never ask for a full card number or security code.”

Customer: “My bank app shows two amounts for the same order.” Which action should come first?

- A: Refund both amounts immediately
- B: Ask for the customer's full card number and security code
- C: Check whether the amounts are pending authorizations or completed charges
- D: Tell the customer to dispute the payment before checking

Answer: C. Explanation: Establish the relevant facts before refunding or escalating. Financial-account details must not be requested unnecessarily.

### P3: prioritization / medium

Policy card: “Suspected account takeover receives immediate escalation to the security team. A normal return request has a one-business-day response target. A general product question has a two-business-day response target.”

Three tickets arrive together. Which should be handled first?

- A: A customer asks which colour a backpack comes in
- B: A customer says their account email changed without permission
- C: A customer asks how to return an unopened item
- D: Handle whichever ticket is shortest to answer

Answer: B. Explanation: An unrequested account-email change signals possible takeover and has immediate priority under the supplied policy.

### P4: returns eligibility / easy

Policy card: “Unopened items can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Opened items are eligible only if faulty. Faulty-item claims go to product support; do not automatically reject them.”

Customer: “I opened the headphones 10 days after delivery. One side never worked.” What should you do?

- A: Reject the claim because the box is open
- B: Route the faulty-item claim to product support
- C: Require the customer to wait until day 30
- D: Tell them to buy another pair

Answer: B. Explanation: The faulty-item exception applies; an opened box does not automatically make the claim ineligible.

### P5: uncertain answer / medium

Policy card: “Agents may confirm only information in the current knowledge base. If an answer is missing, contact the product team and send a progress update within one business day.”

Customer: “Will this accessory work with a device that launched yesterday?” The device is not listed in the knowledge base. Best response?

- A: Say yes because earlier models were compatible
- B: Explain that you will check with the product team and update them within one business day
- C: Say no because the device is new
- D: Close the ticket until the customer contacts you again

Answer: B. Explanation: Acknowledge uncertainty, obtain authoritative information and set the approved follow-up expectation.

### P6: cancellation / medium

Policy card: “An order can be cancelled only while its status is Processing. Dispatched orders cannot be cancelled, but unopened goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery.”

Customer asks to cancel. Status is Dispatched. What should you do?

- A: Promise a cancellation and immediate refund
- B: Explain the dispatch limitation and provide the return option
- C: Change the status to Processing so cancellation becomes available
- D: Say no and end the conversation without alternatives

Answer: B. Explanation: Apply the current status honestly and offer the available next step without inventing authority.

## Original item bank: typing

T1 and T2 are long enough for a 90-second sample. Display plain text without smart punctuation transformations. Allow Backspace and ordinary corrections. Start with an explicit button after instructions and the practice check; use a monotonic timer. Do not require candidates to type the entire passage. Explain that case, punctuation, digits and spaces count. Mark paste as “review needed” if blocked/detected, not proof of cheating; assistive technology can generate unusual input events.

### T1: order update

“Thank you for contacting our support team. I have checked the tracking information for order 4827, and the parcel is still with the delivery partner. The last update was recorded on Tuesday at 10:30. I will open an investigation today and send you an update within two business days. You do not need to place a second order while we check the delivery. If you receive the parcel before our next message, please reply to this email so we can close the investigation. We cannot confirm an arrival date until the delivery partner responds. Your original payment and delivery details will remain unchanged during this process. Please do not send your full card number, password, or security code. If we need more information, we will explain exactly what is required and provide a secure way to share it. You can find your current order status in the account section of our website. Select Orders, choose order 4827, and open the tracking link. If the link does not load, let us know which browser you are using and whether an error message appears. We understand that a delayed delivery can be frustrating, especially when the item is needed for a specific date. Our next update will explain what we have learned and the options available to you. Thank you for your patience while we work with the delivery partner to resolve the issue.”

### T2: return instructions

“Your return request has been approved. Please place the unopened item in a secure box and include the order reference 7351 inside the parcel. Remove or cover any old shipping labels before attaching the new return label. The label will be sent to your account email within one business day. You can print it at home or use the code at a participating drop-off point. Keep the receipt from the drop-off point until we confirm that your refund has been processed. When the parcel reaches our warehouse, the team will check the item and update your return status. This inspection normally takes two business days. If everything meets the return policy, your refund will be sent to the original payment method. Your payment provider may then take up to five business days to show the amount in your account. We cannot send the refund to a different card or bank account. If you have not received the label after one business day, please check your spam folder and reply to this message. You do not need to submit a second return request. If the item arrived damaged, tell us before sending it back so we can give you the correct instructions. Please describe the damage in a few words and include a photo of the item if requested through our secure upload link. Never include payment details in a photograph. We will help you choose the safest next step.”

### Typing scoring specification

This is a transparent proposed implementation, not a claim to reproduce a vendor's proprietary NWPM formula.

1. Use final submitted text, not every keypress, so correction keystrokes do not inflate speed. Let t be elapsed active minutes, N the final output's character count, and M the exact character matches in an edit-distance alignment against the attempted prefix of the source. Include spaces, punctuation, case and digits. Normalize only CRLF/LF and Unicode canonical composition; do not silently ignore case or punctuation.
2. Align output with a source prefix using Levenshtein operations. Let S, I and D be substitutions, insertions and interior deletions. Choose the minimum-distance prefix alignment; prefer the longer prefix when costs tie. Ignore the unattempted source tail. Otherwise a slower but accurate candidate would be incorrectly penalized for not finishing the passage.
3. Gross WPM = N / (5 × t). Correct-character WPM = M / (5 × t). Label the latter “Adjusted WPM” with an explanation; it is not universally defined NWPM.
4. Accuracy = 100 × M / (M + S + I + D). Empty attempts have zero speed and zero accuracy, labelled “No typing submitted,” rather than NaN or a misleading 100%.
5. Pilot typing-module score = 0.40 × min(100, Adjusted WPM / targetWPM × 100) + 0.60 × accuracy. Default targetWPM 45 is a configurable example only, not a validated universal minimum. Keep raw speed and accuracy prominent even when displaying the combined score.
6. Do not count an untyped tail as errors. Do count skipped words inside the attempted section. Show character-level source/output evidence, elapsed time, corrections if measured, and the scoring formula/version.

Example: N=300, M=294, S=6, I=D=0, t=1.5 gives gross 40.0 WPM, adjusted 39.2 WPM and 98.0% accuracy. With an illustrative 45-WPM target the module score is 93.6/100 and contributes 14.0/15. A missing trial has no reliable result and should request human review rather than silently become a complete overall score.

For an initial prototype, a simpler positional character comparison is acceptable only if clearly labelled an approximation: one missed character shifts later text and makes it unfair. Prefer the alignment method before live applicant use.

## Original item bank: written customer response

Target length is a guideline, not an automatic penalty. Candidates may write more or less if they cover the customer's need. Give four minutes in the default route. Accept equivalent phrasing; the sample is not the sole valid answer. Evaluate what the response says, not a count of empathy keywords or sentiment.

### W1: delayed order (default)

Customer: “I ordered a gift for this weekend. Tracking hasn't changed for four business days. This is really frustrating. Can you send another one today or guarantee delivery tomorrow?”

Policy supplied: “Open a carrier investigation after three business days without a tracking update. Investigations take up to two business days. Replacements and refunds may be offered after an investigation confirms the parcel is lost. Agents cannot guarantee a delivery date or issue a replacement before that outcome. The agent can provide a progress update within two business days.”

Task: Write a helpful reply of approximately 70–120 words. Acknowledge the concern, explain the next action and timeframe, and avoid promises the policy does not support.

Strong sample: “I'm sorry the delivery delay has put your gift plans at risk. Since tracking has not changed for four business days, I'll open an investigation with the delivery partner today. I'll update you within two business days. I can't confirm delivery tomorrow or send a replacement before we have the investigation result, but if the parcel is confirmed lost, we can review the replacement or refund options. If it arrives before our next update, please let us know. I understand this may not give you the certainty you need for the weekend, and I'll keep you informed as soon as we hear back.”

Required evidence: recognizes emotional/time context; investigation threshold met; investigation action; update within two business days; no delivery guarantee; no premature replacement/refund; conditional options are accurately described.

### W2: duplicate payment

Customer: “There are two payments for the same order in my bank app. I need my money back now.”

Policy supplied: “Check whether the amounts are pending authorizations or completed charges. Pending authorizations normally disappear within five business days, but the bank controls the timing. If two completed charges are confirmed for one order, escalate to billing with both transaction IDs; billing reviews within one business day. Do not request a full card number or security code.”

Task: Write approximately 60–100 words explaining the check and next steps without assuming a refund is already approved.

Strong sample: “I understand why seeing two amounts is worrying. I'll first check whether both are completed charges or whether one is a pending authorization. A pending authorization normally disappears within five business days, although your bank controls the exact timing. If there are two completed charges for this order, I'll send the transaction details to our billing team for review within one business day and keep you updated. Please don't send your full card number or security code. I'll explain any further information we need through our secure support process.”

Required evidence: checks status; distinguishes authorization/charge; conditional billing escalation; correct review timeframe; no guaranteed refund/date; avoids requesting card secrets.

### W3: cancellation after dispatch

Customer: “I ordered the wrong size. Cancel order 7351 right away, please.”

Order status: Dispatched.

Policy supplied: “Cancellation is available only while an order is Processing. Dispatched orders cannot be cancelled. Unopened items can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. The customer can start a return from Orders in their account; a label is sent within one business day.”

Task: Write approximately 60–100 words explaining what can happen next.

Strong sample: “I understand you want to stop the order because the size is wrong. Order 7351 has already been dispatched, so I can't cancel it at this stage. You can return the item if it remains unopened and you start the return within 30 days of delivery. Once it arrives, open Orders in your account, select order 7351, and choose Start a return. The return label will be sent within one business day. Please keep the item unopened while you decide. If you have trouble starting the return, reply here and we'll help.”

Required evidence: accurate status and cancellation limit; unopened condition; 30-day delivery-based window; account steps and label timeframe; helpful tone; no invented free return/exchange/refund promise.

### Human writing rubric: 20 raw points, scaled to 40 weighted points

| Dimension | Maximum | Full-credit evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Policy accuracy and safe judgment | 6 | Correct policy, conditions and facts; no invented guarantee, authority or unsafe data request |
| Action and expectation setting | 5 | Clear next step/owner and supplied timeframe; customer knows what happens next |
| Empathy and professionalism | 3 | Specific acknowledgement of concern, respectful tone and appropriate ownership |
| Clarity and concision | 3 | Readable structure, plain language, direct explanation without needless jargon |
| Spelling and grammar | 3 | Mechanics support understanding; legitimate regional spelling accepted |

For each dimension: 0 means absent/incorrect; approximately half credit means partly demonstrated; full credit means the stated evidence is clearly present. Reviewers can award intermediate integer values with a note. A serious policy error creates a visible “Policy-risk review” flag, but no automatic rejection. Do not double-count one typo across several dimensions.

Every writing score must have short evidence notes and a reviewer identity/date. Initially leave writing “Awaiting human review.” If a demo offers heuristic guidance, label it “Draft indicator, not a hiring score.” Never present keyword matching as AI semantic evaluation. Recruiter overrides need a reason and an audit entry. A second reviewer should check borderline/high-risk decisions.

## Explainable result card

- Status: submitted / incomplete / technical issue / awaiting writing review / human review complete
- Assessment title, client/role, version, modules, durations and accommodations applied (without diagnosis)
- Mechanics: correct count/total and each selected answer with its explanation
- Typing: elapsed time, gross WPM, adjusted WPM, accuracy and source/output evidence
- Problem solving: correct count/total; decision and policy rationale for each scenario
- Writing: full response, per-dimension human marks and evidence, policy-risk flag and reviewer notes
- Weighted score only when all required components are valid and writing is reviewed; otherwise “Partial results” and the completed weight, not an apparently final total
- Next action chosen by a human: interview / second work sample / clarification / no further action; record rationale, never default an automatic rejection
- Export a report containing responses, rubric, settings/version and reviewer decisions; avoid demographic guesses, personality labels, protected-trait inference or unsupported confidence/percentile claims

## Fairness, accessibility and deployment cautions

1. Tie tasks and thresholds to the actual role, client writing language and tools. A ten-minute screen samples little evidence. Pilot with incumbent agents and known new hires, compare results with job performance, review question difficulty, and revise using documented evidence before using hard cutoffs. Avoid personality, accent, school pedigree and generic IQ as substitutes for support work.
2. Offer an accommodation route before the timer begins, keyboard navigation, labelled inputs, visible focus, readable contrast, text resizing and screen-reader-readable policy cards. Do not repeatedly announce a changing timer every second. Preserve access to reference material and do not force full-screen or webcam surveillance for a low-stakes work sample.
3. Separate a genuinely job-relevant timed typing measure from reading/reasoning/writing time limits. Allow recruiter-approved extra time or an equivalent accessible route without penalizing the applicant. For W3C timing guidance, the general expectation is the ability to disable, adjust or extend time limits, subject to exceptions; test timing can be controlled by a third party. Do not assert a universal “essential” exemption for every module. Source: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html
4. In the U.S., EEOC guidance explains that employment tests can raise discrimination concerns if they disproportionately exclude protected groups, and that job relevance/validation and accommodations matter. Do not assume vendor compliance marketing makes this custom instrument lawful in every jurisdiction. Sources: https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/employment-tests-and-selection-procedures and https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/job-applicants-and-ada
5. Score authorized assistive tools consistently with the job's intended construct. Do not infer dishonesty from fast entry, focus changes, paste events, a browser warning or an assistive-input pattern. Retakes, connectivity interruptions and accommodation handling need human review.
6. Inform candidates of the purpose, expected duration, allowed tools, data recipients, retention and how to request help. Collect only what is necessary; no medical diagnosis, birth date, financial data, government ID or webcam image is needed for this prototype. Fictional prompts must never include a real customer's private record.
7. A static/browser-only prototype must not be described as secure, validated, production-ready or a real multi-tenant applicant-tracking system. Before handling live candidate data, implement authentication, role-based authorization, encrypted transport, storage/retention controls, audit logging, privacy notice and a defined human hiring process.
